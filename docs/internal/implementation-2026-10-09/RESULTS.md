## Architecture and missing requirements

The kernel should own intent/admission, frozen work and verification criteria, evidence identity/provenance, dependency invalidation, relevance records, permission boundaries, leases and trust transitions. Adapters own actual source access, principal/auth probes, observed tool results, model processes, commands, clocks and delivery sinks. Versioned methods describe when and how to reason; an installed skill must not impersonate an executor or permission grant.

The next implementation-ready children are native `work-bc13f708` (non-code verifier/routing plus quantitative and action-scope completeness), `work-0b8e5dbe` (claim-specific source authority), and `work-2260468a` (reviewer execution-evidence transport and actual composed methods). Add unseen renamed domains, repeated fresh installs, permission-denied/partial/empty variants, same-timestamp corrections, interrupted leases, real scoped API/MCP traversal, conditional lenses and disposable schedule/overlap/cancel/restart delivery checks. Each pass must include the actual artifact and lifecycle, not only routing or method labels.
