Mallow Seed Club — trial protocol T-17, recorded 10 September 2026
All four trays used lot M-17. Each tray contained 100 seeds. D1 and D2 were dry-sown; S1 and S2 received the overnight pre-soak. All trays were sown on 10 September and counted on day 10 using the same criterion: an emerged seedling. No trays or seeds were excluded from the recorded trial.
D1 and D2 stayed on the west bench. S1 and S2 stayed on the east bench. Bench assignment was not randomized or rotated. Bench temperature and light measurements were not collected.
The grow lead maintains count-events.jsonl. Each event's count is the whole-tray count, not an increment. A correction's supersedes field replaces the named earlier event. The log is complete for this trial as of 21 September.
This packet contains no repeat trial or independent count audit.
