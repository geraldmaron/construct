SYNTHETIC RECORD A1 — Approved pilot measurement protocol
Document signed 3 February 2026 by Eastbridge Markets and the collection contractor.
Evaluation covers Eastbank and Ridgeway markets, comparing their four September Saturday trading dates in 2025 and 2026. Harbour market opened in 2026 and is outside the matched pilot evaluation.
Residual waste means the material weighed for landfill collection; separated compost is outside this measure. Net waste kilograms = gross loaded-container kilograms minus the recorded empty-container tare. Do not treat gross container weight as waste.
Measure: combined net residual waste kg per 100 vendor-days across both matched markets. A vendor-day is one stall trading on one market date, irrespective of hours or sales. Combine totals before calculating the rate.
Target is met when the measured rate is at least 20.0% below the matched 2025 rate. Exactly 20.0% meets the target.
The comparison measures observed waste intensity. Without a comparison group or controls it does not establish which part of any change the programme caused.
