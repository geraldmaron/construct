SYNTHETIC RECORD B2 — Signed museum intake receipt, dated 6 February 1931
Received from Helena Vale: one cedar survey chest, brass plate NV-17, crescent-shaped repair to lid.
Basis: temporary loan. Receipt number L31.6. Lender retains title until a separate transfer is accepted by the museum. This receipt is not a purchase or donation agreement.
Condition signed by lender Helena Vale and stores clerk: sound with old lid repair.
Reverse: moved to gallery for installation 14 March 1931. Signed by gallery keeper that day.
The receipt contains no Captain Rowan Vale signature and no statement of Helena Vale's relationship to the captain.
