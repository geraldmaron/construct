SYNTHETIC RECORD B3 — Executed sale agreement, signatures dated 21 August 1934
Seller: Helena Vale. Buyer: Northmere Museum Trust. Item: cedar survey chest, brass plate NV-17, previously received as temporary loan L31.6.
Price: £18. Seller acknowledges payment in full on signature.
Transfer clause: title transfers upon written acceptance by the museum trustees; payment and physical custody alone do not constitute acceptance. Until that acceptance the existing loan terms remain in effect.
No captain is a named party to this agreement.
