SYNTHETIC RECORD B5 — Bound gallery location register, dated event entries
14 March 1931: Chest NV-17 installation completed; opened to public view with the gallery at noon.
9 November 1942: Chest NV-17 removed from gallery to secured store during roof works. Not on public view.
18 January 1943: Chest NV-17 returned from store, gallery display reopened.
These are the original signed entries at those dates, not a continuous public-access attendance log. The enclosed extract includes all chest location entries available in the described surviving volume.
