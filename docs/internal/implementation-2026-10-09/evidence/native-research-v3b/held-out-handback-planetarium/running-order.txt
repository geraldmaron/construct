Larkfield Astronomy Circle — producer's running order, 8 October 2026
The held programme requires 10 minutes of preparation before the first audience enters, 25 minutes per show including entry and questions, 10 minutes of changeover between consecutive shows, and 10 minutes of clearance after the last show. There is no changeover after the last show in addition to clearance. These activities are sequential and cannot overlap. The programme has not been shortened or retimed.
The running order defines programme duration. The venue bookings desk defines room access; the current venue layout defines available audience places.
