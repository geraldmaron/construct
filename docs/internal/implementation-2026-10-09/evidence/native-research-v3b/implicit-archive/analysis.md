# Archive pilot: held-record feasibility assessment

Assessment date: October 9, 2026. Status: assessment complete; the proposed full public release is not supported by the held records. This is an evidence assessment, not approval to scan or publish.

## Finding and recommendation

**Inference: do not approve the proposal as written.** It overstates available operator time, its own scanning estimate does not cover the proposed collection, and the recorded clearance covers only a smaller internal pilot, not public distribution. [cite: proposal.md; operations.md; rights.md]

**Inference: a pilot limited to the 120 internally cleared photographs is conditionally feasible for scanning, but end-to-end completion is unproven.** At the observed scanning rate, it leaves four operator-hours for setup, metadata and any other work within the recorded allocation. Recommend this narrower internal scope for consideration, without treating the recommendation as execution permission. [cite: operations.md; rights.md]

It does not determine legal rights or examine the photographs themselves. [cite: proposal.md; operations.md; rights.md]

For staffing, the operational statement is more directly scoped to the allocation than the proposal's assumption; for permitted scope, the rights statement is more relevant than the proposal's requested public release. ## Capacity reconciliation

Calculations below are **inferences**, assume the sample rate applies throughout, and use aggregate operator-hours rather than elapsed hours. Setup and metadata are outside that rate. The completion boundary is enough capacity for every photograph in the selected scope, with all required work fitting the same allocation. [cite: operations.md]

| Comparison | Source quantities and calculation | Distance from completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal's own scan-only case | 2 operators × 12 hours = 24 operator-hours; 24 × 15 photographs/hour = 360 photographs | 600 − 360 = 240 photographs short, even on its own assumption. [cite: proposal.md] |
| Held allocation, full collection | 12 total operator-hours × 15 photographs/hour = 180 photographs, before excluded work | 600 − 180 = 420 photographs short; this is nominal scan-only capacity, not a delivery commitment. [cite: operations.md; proposal.md] |

**Inference:** two staff do not double an allocation explicitly recorded as shared. The four-hour remainder is an allowance, not measured overhead or demonstrated slack. These calculations establish neither a staffing authorization nor a reliable calendar completion date. [cite: operations.md; proposal.md]

## Strongest objection

**Inference:** these brief, undated statements may omit later staffing or permission changes, so the assessment cannot prove that a future revised project is impossible. Nevertheless, unseen changes cannot justify this proposal, and even its own capacity calculation falls short of its stated collection size. The best supported alternative is the cleared internal subset, accepted only with the stated scope and effort conditions. [cite: proposal.md; operations.md; rights.md]

## Pre-mortem

**Inference:** the narrower pilot's most plausible failure is treating scan-only throughput as complete processing throughput: setup and metadata exhaust the four-hour remainder. A separate scope failure would be treating internal clearance as public-release permission. The assessment controls these risks by withholding a completion promise and keeping public distribution outside the recommended pilot. [cite: operations.md; rights.md]
