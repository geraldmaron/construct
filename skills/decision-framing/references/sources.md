# Method sources

Method identified, not incorporated - reading these is not required:

- Klein, "Performing a Project Premortem," Harvard Business Review,
  September 2007 - prospective hindsight.
- Nygard, "Documenting Architecture Decisions," 2011 - context, decision,
  and consequences a later reader can audit.
- Bezos, Amazon shareholder letter, 1997 (restated 2015) - Type 1 / Type 2
  decisions; reversibility sets process weight.
- Spetzler, Winter & Meyer, *Decision Quality*, Wiley 2016 - frame,
  alternatives, and clear values as separable elements.
- Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, 1999 - rival hypotheses to
  equal depth rather than confirming the first.
