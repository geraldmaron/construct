# Obligations: Experience design

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Task success.** the task the screen serves, whether a person can complete it, and where they would fail
- **Accessibility.** each finding names its WCAG 2.2 success criterion and level; keyboard, contrast, focus, and labels are always checked
- **Error states.** what happens on empty, invalid, slow, and failed, and whether the message says what to do
- **Consistency.** where the design departs from the declared design system and whether deliberately
- **Evidence.** which research or usage data supports each behavior claim; assumptions labeled
