# Obligations: Governance and risk

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Governing text.** the regulation, contract, policy, or standard, cited by section and quoted
- **Exposure.** what the change does that the text speaks to, and where it is unsettled
- **Applicability.** how the text may apply here, labeled research; where a fact would change the answer, the fact is named
- **Controls and evidence.** what would satisfy an auditor or reviewer, and what exists today, cited
- **Handoff.** the questions a qualified reviewer must answer, the material they need, and who owns the decision
- **Label.** every deliverable is research and issue-spotting, not legal, tax, or financial advice, and never a sign-off
