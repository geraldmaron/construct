# Sources: Governance and risk

Doctrine this pack rests on. Each entry names what it is used for and when it was last reviewed. Each entry was opened on 2026-10-17 and says what was confirmed; a finding that leans on a specific clause or number still opens that clause. Time-sensitive doctrine carries a review-due date.

- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), full text (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj). Used for: locating articles a data-flow question turns on; quoted, never paraphrased into a conclusion. Reviewed: 2026-09-02, review due 2027-03. Opened 2026-10-17: EUR-Lex serves the full text in English at this address, with articles quotable in place (for example Article 5 on processing principles and Article 25 on data protection by design and by default).
- AICPA Trust Services Criteria (SOC 2) (https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022). Used for: the control vocabulary an auditor expects (security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy). Reviewed: 2026-09-02, review due 2027-03. Opened 2026-10-17: AICPA & CIMA publish the 2017 Trust Services Criteria with revised points of focus (2022), set by the Assurance Services Executive Committee, covering those five categories and tied to SOC 2.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (https://www.iso.org/standard/27001). Used for: control objectives and evidence expectations. Reviewed: 2026-09-02, review due 2027-09. Opened 2026-10-17: the landing page confirms ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Edition 3, published 2022-10, as the ISMS requirements standard, with Amd 1:2024 (climate action changes); the control text itself is sold and was not opened.
- COSO Enterprise Risk Management framework (https://www.coso.org/guidance-erm). Used for: the vocabulary of risk appetite, controls, and evidence. Reviewed: 2026-09-02; stable. Opened 2026-10-17: COSO lists the 2017 update, Enterprise Risk Management: Integrating with Strategy and Performance, which replaced the 2004 framework, alongside guidance on risk appetite (2012 and 2020).
- Sarbanes-Oxley Act section 404 (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-107publ204/html/PLAW-107publ204.htm) and PCAOB AS 2201 (https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201). Used for: internal-control-over-financial-reporting expectations where finance processes change; issue spotting only. Reviewed: 2026-09-02, review due 2027-09. Opened 2026-10-17: Public Law 107-204 section 404 requires a management assessment of internal control over financial reporting in the annual report and an auditor attestation to it; AS 2201 governs that audit, and amendments to paragraph .09 and a new .99 take effect 15 December 2026.
- FASB ASC 606, Revenue from Contracts with Customers (https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202014-09_Section%20A.pdf). Used for: spotting revenue-recognition questions when money flows change; treatment is an accountant's judgment. Reviewed: 2026-09-02, review due 2027-09. Opened 2026-10-17: FASB's ASU 2014-09 (May 2014) creates Topic 606 and states its core principle that revenue depicts the transfer of promised goods or services at the consideration the entity expects; later amending updates were not opened.
