# Obligations: Operations and reliability

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Objectives.** the SLOs and error budget, with the measurement that feeds them; unmeasured where none exists
- **Signals and alerts.** what fires, on what symptom, to whom, and what noise is expected
- **On-call.** who is paged, the rotation, and what they can do at that hour
- **Runbooks.** tested or untested, for each expected failure
- **Rollback and capacity.** how to undo the change and how much headroom exists
- **Learning.** for an incident: timeline, contributing factors, and actions with owners, without blame
