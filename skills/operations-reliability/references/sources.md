# Sources: Operations and reliability

Doctrine this pack rests on. Each entry names what it is used for and when it was last reviewed. Each entry was opened on 2026-10-17 and says what was confirmed; a finding that leans on a specific clause or number still opens that clause. Time-sensitive doctrine carries a review-due date.

- Beyer et al., Site Reliability Engineering (https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/). Used for: SLOs, error budgets, monitoring's four golden signals, on-call, and blameless postmortems. Reviewed: 2026-09-02; stable. Opened 2026-10-17: the free online book has chapters on service level objectives (4), monitoring distributed systems (6), being on-call (11), and postmortem culture (15); chapter 6 (https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/) names the four golden signals as latency, traffic, errors, and saturation.
- Beyer et al., The Site Reliability Workbook (https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/). Used for: SLO engineering practice and alerting on symptoms. Reviewed: 2026-09-02; stable. Opened 2026-10-17: the workbook has chapters "Implementing SLOs" (2) and "Alerting on SLOs" (5).
- Allspaw, Blameless PostMortems and a Just Culture (https://www.etsy.com/codeascraft/blameless-postmortems). Used for: the posture and structure of an incident review. Reviewed: 2026-09-02; stable. Opened 2026-10-17: the post by John Allspaw, 22 May 2012, describes a just culture balancing safety and accountability, and a postmortem where engineers give a detailed account of actions, effects, expectations, assumptions, and timeline without fear of punishment.
- PagerDuty Incident Response documentation (https://response.pagerduty.com/). Used for: roles during an incident and after. Reviewed: 2026-09-02, review due 2027-03. Opened 2026-10-17: the documentation defines incident roles including incident commander, deputy, scribe, subject matter expert, and liaisons, and has an "After an Incident" section with a postmortem process and template.

Used for: stability patterns (timeouts, circuit breakers, bulkheads) a readiness review looks for. Reviewed: 2026-09-02; stable.
