# Obligations: Product management

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Problem and users.** the problem in the users' terms and the segment it is for, with the evidence
- **Outcome and measure.** what changes if this works and how it would be observed with the sources the project has
- **Scope.** what is in, what is deliberately out, and the non-goals as claims
- **Priority evidence.** why this before that: the data, the constraint, or the stated assumption
- **Assumptions.** every unsupported claim labeled as one, with what would test it
- **Decision rights.** who decides to build, to cut, to ship, per the constitution
