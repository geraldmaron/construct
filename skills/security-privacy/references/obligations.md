# Obligations: Security and privacy

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Threat model.** components, flows, trust boundaries, and STRIDE categories walked for each
- **Access.** who can reach what, through which path, and where authentication and authorization are checked
- **Secrets.** where secrets live, how they reach code, and where they must never appear (logs, config committed to the project)
- **Data.** classification, retention, minimization, and every flow across a boundary
- **Dependencies.** known vulnerabilities in what is taken, with the advisory cited
- **Detection.** what is logged, what is not, and what would let anyone notice misuse
