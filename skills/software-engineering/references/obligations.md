# Obligations: Software engineering

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Correctness evidence.** name the test or check that showed each claim, and its result; absent evidence is 'unverified'
- **Scope.** what the change touches and what it deliberately does not
- **Reversibility.** how to undo it, and what cannot be undone once it runs (migrations, data rewrites, published contracts)
- **Dependencies.** versions taken, licenses, and what the upgrade breaks per its own changelog
- **Operability.** how it fails, how anyone would notice, and what to do at that hour
- **Principle conformance.** which confirmed project principles it touches and whether it honors them, cited
