# Obligations: Strategy and research

What a deliverable this pack owns must carry, as checkable slots. Empty slots are named as empty, never filled with invention.

- **Kernel.** the strategy's diagnosis, guiding policy, and coherent actions, quoted; missing parts named
- **Normalization.** initiatives, owners, dates, dependencies, allocations, constraints, skills, operational load, and throughput history, each cited to a source with its declared authority for that claim type
- **Deterministic conflicts.** double allocations, dates before dependencies, initiatives with no owner, no work, no measure, work with no initiative; found by check, not by reading
- **Capacity.** ranges from explicit assumptions; history is evidence, never the figure
- **Semantic conflicts.** incompatible commitments found by review, each cited to both texts; similarity is not contradiction
- **Decisions.** recommended calls handed to the owners the constitution names
