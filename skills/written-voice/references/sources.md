# Method sources

Method identified, not incorporated - reading these is not required:

- Minto, *The Pyramid Principle*, 1987 - governing thought first, support
  beneath.
- Gopen & Swan, "The Science of Scientific Writing," American Scientist
  78(6), 1990 - readers take meaning from where information sits.
- Williams & Bizup, *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace* - clarity as
  characters-and-actions; hedges and throat-clearing as defects.
- Plain Writing Act of 2010, and U.S. federal plain-language guidelines -
  the reader's action as organizing principle.
- Orwell, "Politics and the English Language," 1946 - inflated vocabulary
  as a way of not saying things.
- Mayer, *Multimedia Learning*, 3rd ed., 2021 - segmenting and signaling.
- Cowan, "The magical number 4 in short-term memory," BBS 2001 - why no
  seven-item ceiling ships as science.
- McDowell et al., registered randomized trial of tabular fact boxes,
  2020 (PMC7137953) - tables over prose for comparison data.
- Geiger & Downen, "The Effect of Structure on Memory for Procedural
  Text," Psychological Reports 2021 - lists beat prose for re-use, not
  first-pass reading.
- Kobak et al., "Delving into LLM-assisted writing," Science Advances -
  measured lexical tics.
- Wood, Isenberg et al., "Sketchy Rendering for Information
  Visualization," IEEE TVCG 2012, and its 2025 follow-up - conditional
  case for hand-drawn diagram style.
- Nielsen Norman Group, F-shaped scanning research - front-loading, with
  its descriptive-not-prescriptive caveat.
